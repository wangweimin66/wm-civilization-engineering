# Moc Bai Business Plan (E-01)

## 1. Objective（目标）

Build a cross-border business system leveraging Vietnam’s economic nodes and border trade advantages.

---

## 2. Strategic Basis（策略依据）

From system analysis:

- Country: Vietnam
- Risk Level: B
- Strategy: Economic Entry
- Target Node: Border Trade + Supply Chain

---

## 3. Core Opportunity（核心机会）

### 3.1 Location Advantage
- Moc Bai = border node (Vietnam ↔ Cambodia)
- High flow of goods and people

---

### 3.2 System Insight
- Border nodes = low control + high flow
- Ideal for entry-level control

---

## 4. Business Model（商业模式）

### Model A: Cross-Border Supply Agent

- Source products from China
- Supply to Vietnam / Cambodia market

---

### Model B: Gray Zone Services

- Logistics support
- Currency exchange
- Local coordination

---

### Model C: Tourist-Driven Sales

- Serve casino / border visitors
- High-margin fast-moving goods

---

## 5. Execution Plan（执行步骤）

### Phase 1: Entry（进入）

- Identify local partners
- Test small-scale trading
- Build supply channels

---

### Phase 2: Expansion（扩展）

- Increase product range
- Establish stable logistics
- Build repeat customers

---

### Phase 3: Control（控制）

- Lock supply sources
- Build local influence network
- Expand to multiple nodes

---

## 6. Cost Structure（成本）

- Initial capital: ~$10,000
- Logistics
- Local operations
- Risk buffer

---

## 7. Risk Control（风险控制）

### Political Risk：
- Avoid sensitive areas

### Economic Risk：
- Fast turnover strategy

### Operational Risk：
- Use local partners

---

## 8. Exit Strategy（退出机制）

- Liquidate inventory quickly
- Withdraw capital
- Transfer operations

---

## 9. System Integration（系统整合）

- Based on Node System
- Driven by Evaluation Model
- Guided by Strategy Layer

---

## 10. Future Expansion（扩展）

- Replicate to other border nodes
- Build multi-country network
