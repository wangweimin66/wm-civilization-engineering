# Global Strategy Engine

## 1. Purpose（目的）

Define how resources (capital, people, information) should be allocated globally based on the World Mapping Engine.

---

## 2. Core Logic（核心逻辑）

Global allocation is based on:

- Country Type
- Node Strength
- Risk Level

---

## 3. Resource Types（资源类型）

### 3.1 Capital（资本）
- 投资资金
- 流动资金

---

### 3.2 People（人口）
- 操作人员
- 管理层
- 本地代理

---

### 3.3 Information（信息）
- 数据
- 渠道
- 影响力

---

## 4. Allocation Strategy（资源配置策略）

---

### 4.1 Growth Systems（增长型国家）

👉 Allocation:

- High capital allocation
- Long-term presence
- Build core nodes

---

### 4.2 Fragmented Systems（碎片型国家）

👉 Allocation:

- Medium capital
- Selective city entry
- Flexible structure

---

### 4.3 Volatile Systems（波动型国家）

👉 Allocation:

- Low capital
- Short-term deployment
- Arbitrage focus

---

## 5. Flow Strategy（流动策略）

### Capital Flow：

- From stable base → Growth Systems  
- Avoid long-term lock in Volatile Systems  

---

### People Flow：

- Core team → stable regions  
- Local agents → fragmented regions  

---

### Information Flow：

- Centralized control  
- Distributed channels  

---

## 6. Risk Balancing（风险平衡）

### Portfolio Structure：

- 60% Growth Systems  
- 30% Fragmented Systems  
- 10% Volatile Systems  

---

## 7. Control Model（控制模型）

- Control key nodes in Growth Systems  
- Maintain flexibility in Fragmented Systems  
- Avoid control attempts in Volatile Systems  

---

## 8. System Integration（系统整合）

- Input：World Mapping Engine  
- Output：Global Resource Allocation Plan  

---

## 9. Execution Interface（执行接口）

Connect to:

- Market Entry Strategy  
- Business Plans  
- AI Systems  

---

## 10. Future Expansion（扩展）

- Dynamic capital allocation  
- AI-driven global optimization  
- Real-time risk adjustment
