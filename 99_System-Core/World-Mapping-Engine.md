# World Mapping Engine

## 1. Purpose（目的）

Map global regions based on the Country Types Model to understand macro-level civilization structures and strategic entry zones.

---

## 2. Global Classification（全球分类）

### 2.1 Growth Systems（增长型）

Regions:
- Southeast Asia
- Parts of East Asia

Examples:
- Vietnam
- Indonesia
- Thailand

---

### 2.2 Volatile Systems（波动型）

Regions:
- Latin America
- Some Middle East areas

Examples:
- Argentina
- Venezuela

---

### 2.3 Fragmented Systems（碎片型）

Regions:
- South Asia
- Africa
- Large developing countries

Examples:
- India
- Nigeria

---

## 3. Strategic Map（战略地图）

### High Priority Zones（优先区域）
- Growth Systems

👉 Strategy:
- Deep entry
- Long-term investment

---

### Medium Zones（中等区域）
- Fragmented Systems

👉 Strategy:
- Node-based entry
- City-level operations

---

### High Risk Zones（高风险区域）
- Volatile Systems

👉 Strategy:
- Short-term operations
- Arbitrage only

---

## 4. Flow Logic（流动逻辑）

Global flow pattern:

- Capital → Growth Systems
- Arbitrage → Volatile Systems
- Selective Entry → Fragmented Systems

---

## 5. Control Strategy（控制策略）

- 控制 Growth Nodes → 长期收益
- 利用 Volatile Nodes → 短期收益
- 渗透 Fragmented Nodes → 中期布局
